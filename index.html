The medinas

UNESCO listed · 1979

Tunis

The largest. 700 monuments, from palaces to mosques, and the El Attarine souk scented with jasmine and amber.

Landmark: the Zitouna Mosque, heart of the maze.

UNESCO listed · 1988

Sfax

The most authentic. Barely touristic, with intact ramparts — trade here still works the way it did in the 9th century.

Landmark: Bab Diwan, the main gate.

UNESCO listed · 1988

Sousse

The most maritime. The ribat has watched over the harbour since 821, and the kasbah overlooks it all.

Landmark: the ribat and its watchtower.
